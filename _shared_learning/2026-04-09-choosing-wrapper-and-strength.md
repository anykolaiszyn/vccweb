## Wrapper Quick Guide

- Connecticut: cream, cedar, and almond, with a gentle, approachable character
- Habano: medium body with spice and depth
- Maduro: richer and sweeter, with cocoa and dried fruit

## What the Wrapper Tells You

The wrapper is the leaf you taste first, and it shapes the opening impression more than any other
part of the cigar. Body — how much weight the smoke carries on the palate — is a separate question
from flavor, which is what you actually taste. A cigar can be rich in flavor without being heavy in
body, and the other way round.

## Recommendation

Start from the flavors you already enjoy in coffee, chocolate or whiskey and pick the wrapper that
matches: cream and nuts point to Connecticut, spice to Habano, cocoa and sweetness to Maduro. Smoke
two or three before you decide, and tell the crew what you noticed — that is faster than any chart.
